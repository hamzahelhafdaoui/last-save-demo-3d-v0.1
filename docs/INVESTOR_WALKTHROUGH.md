# Guided development walkthrough

This is a live, early development build. The quality target remains higher than the current art and animation. Use **Story tour** for a forgiving demonstration; Adventure adds a timed crossing; Living quest is an experimental side quest/combat branch.

1. Begin Adventure. Watch or skip the 22-second celestial arrival. Name the character and change folk/fur, forelock, eyes, tunic, scarf and satchel. Drag the scene to turn the character.
2. Walk through the Wayfarer's Room: fireplace, shelves, seating, journal, wardrobe and celestial window. The third world portal is explicitly locked. Take the green Lumenreach portal.
3. Cross the stone bridge into Bellhaven. Speak to Edda near the village entrance and accept **The Last Bell of Lumenreach**.
4. Return across the bridge to the moonflower grove on the western bank. Wake all three large flowers, then welcome the luminous creature and collect kindness.
5. Return to the village side. Turn the ancient crossing mechanism west of Bellhaven. Traverse the three rune stones in order and collect courage at the far end. Story tour provides connecting surfaces; Adventure/Living Quest require jumps and a timed retry is possible.
6. Visit the open archive east of the village. Interact with the lantern, bell and book in that order: light, call, remember. Collect belonging.
7. Approach Crownstone from its southern entrance ramp. Answer kindness, courage and belonging at the three runes. Approach the sword and claim Dawnwake.
8. Watch the authored extraction sequence: reach, resistance, rising blade, fractured stone, light beam and sunrise. The rotating item reveal follows. Dawnwake enters the actual inventory and can be worn.
9. Follow the violet portal beside Bellhaven into Velvet City. The same customized character and sword arrive there. Meet Sable at the boutique and equip the Velvet jacket.
10. Return home via the city portal. Mount Dawnwake at the fireplace. Watch the closing camera sequence, then keep exploring. Inventory, clothes, decorations, quest progress and positions save on this browser/device.

## Living Quest branch

Select Living quest on the title screen or in Settings. Speak with Mira, the healer near the village entrance. Gather moonfern on Elderwood Trail, then return through the grove. Hollow Wardens can ambush the player. **F** strikes, **Q** dodges; the pink ground rings telegraph enemy attacks. Losing returns the player to the trail without deleting the collected herbs. Delivery grants a unique moonfern lantern, physically displayed at home. Combat, navigation and balancing are experimental.

## Presentation controls

- WASD / arrows: movement; Shift: sprint; Ctrl: slow walk; Space: jump.
- Drag: camera; wheel: zoom; E: interact; I: inventory; J: journal; Escape: menu.
- Touch: independent left analog joystick, right camera drag, jump/action buttons. Living Quest also has strike/dodge buttons. Physical mobile devices remain to be validated.
- H toggles the HUD for clean captures.
- Menu → Investor Demo Mode: replay opening, replay earned Dawnwake, reset, return to entrance, export progress, hide HUD.

The source code, authoring scripts and development notes are available in the GitHub repository. The Vercel production alias uses no expiring share token.
