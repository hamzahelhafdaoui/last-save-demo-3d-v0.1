# Asset sources and art status

All characters, equipment, world geometry, effects, dialogue, quest design and music in this repository were authored for this original Last Save prototype. No franchise models, extracted game assets, copyrighted characters, sound recordings or paid APIs are used.

- `public/assets/traveler.glb`: original stylized animal character, generated by `tools/make-traveler.py` in Blender 4.3. Meter units; Y up; +Z forward after glTF conversion; named torso, head, limb and finger pivots. Editable Blender authoring script is included. ~0.5 MB. Export is uncompressed because the installed Blender Draco library is unavailable.
- `src/render/kit.ts`: original Dawnwake mesh, clothing presentation, portal shader, lanterns, banners, particles and prop construction. Dawnwake currently uses authored runtime geometry rather than a standalone GLB; exporting a reviewed final sword asset is a future art task.
- `src/worlds/*` and `src/render/environment.ts`: original procedural environments. These are editable development assets. Architecture, trees, rock forms and furniture require a dedicated art pass before investor presentation.
- `public/audio/*.ogg`: original additive-synthesis chord loops generated by `tools/make-audio.py`, encoded with FFmpeg Vorbis. These are provisional musical sketches, not a finished orchestral soundtrack. Sound effects are original runtime Web Audio synthesis.
- `public/fonts/*`: Cormorant Garamond and Manrope from Google Fonts, redistributed under the SIL Open Font License. The original OFL texts are included alongside the files. Fonts are hosted locally; the game makes no Google Fonts runtime request.
- Software dependencies retain their own licenses in installed packages and the lockfile. React, Three.js, R3F, Drei, Zustand, Howler and GSAP are used through npm. Do not assume that this document relicenses a dependency.

## Art replacement priorities

1. Review the protagonist silhouette, proportions and faces; produce a consistent rig and hand/finger animation set.
2. Replace the procedural extraction pose with reviewed character animation and a final sword/pedestal asset. Preserve the tested timeline and hand-to-hilt behavior.
3. Author a compact Bellhaven kit: curved tiled roofs, layered plaster/stone/wood surfaces, ivy, shop props and unique NPC costumes.
4. Replace generic tree crowns, terrain grain and distant geometry with a coherent Lumenreach material/foliage set and measured LODs.
5. Refine the home exterior, interior furniture, floor materials and celestial vistas as a composed environment.
6. Refine city materials, storefronts, jackets, lighting and true reflection strategy within measured GPU budgets.
7. Compose/source appropriately licensed score, ambience, Foley and cinematic sound. Keep a provenance record for each imported asset.

The current build demonstrates systems and the complete story route. It has not reached the requested premium animated-film presentation standard. No claim of AAA-quality art, mocap, professional sound, native-device 60 FPS or investor readiness is made.
